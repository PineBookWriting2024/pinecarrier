How long does shipping a car take?

Transit time depends on distance, transport type, and carrier availability. Typical shipments take several days to two weeks, with estimated delivery windows provided at booking.

Can I ship personal items in my car?

Yes, you may keep up to 100 lbs of personal items in a box or suitcase in the trunk, ensuring safety and carrier compliance.

Is my vehicle insured during transport?

Yes, every shipment is protected by the carrier’s cargo insurance. Pine Carrier provides additional coverage for extra protection if needed.

Can I choose open or enclosed transport?

Yes, you can select open transport for cost-effective shipping or enclosed transport to protect luxury, classic, or exotic vehicles from weather and road debris.

Do I need to pay upfront for shipping?

No. Payment is only required once a vetted carrier is assigned. This ensures you only pay when your shipment is confirmed and scheduled.
