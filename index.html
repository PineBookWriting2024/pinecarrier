How Much Do Car Transport Companies Charge?

The cost of vehicle shipping varies based on several factors. Pine Carrier provides transparent pricing, so you know what to expect before booking your shipment. Our Locked-In Price Promise ensures no surprise rate increases after your quote.

Prices depend on the type of transport, vehicle size, distance, and season. Whether you choose open or enclosed transport, we match you with a trusted carrier that fits your budget and needs.

Get an instant quote today and see your shipping cost clearly.

Car shipping cost information

Vehicle Size & Weight

Larger or heavier vehicles cost more to ship because they require more space, fuel, and handling effort during transport.

The Car Condition

Inoperable or damaged vehicles require special equipment and extra care, which can increase the overall shipping cost.

Type Of Vehicle

Luxury, classic, or exotic vehicles often need enclosed transport, which is more expensive than standard open carrier shipping.

Shipping Distance

Longer distances naturally increase costs due to fuel, driver time, and logistical planning for safe, on-time delivery.

How do I get a quote for my vehicle?

You can request a free instant quote online or contact our advisors. We provide clear pricing based on vehicle type, transport distance, and preferred service without charging upfront.

How long does vehicle shipping take?

Transit time depends on distance, transport type, and carrier schedules. Typically, shipments take a few days to a couple of weeks. Our advisors give an estimated delivery window when booking.

Can I ship personal items in my vehicle?

Yes, you may keep up to 100 lbs of personal items in a single box or suitcase inside the trunk. This helps maintain safety and carrier compliance.

What is the Locked-In Price Promise?

The Locked-In Price Promise locks in your quoted price for 30 days. If market rates increase during this period, Pine Carrier covers the difference, preventing any surprise charges after booking.

Are my vehicles insured during transport?

Yes, every shipment is protected by the carrier’s cargo insurance. Pine Carrier also provides additional contingent insurance for extra protection in case the carrier’s coverage falls short.

How do I track my vehicle shipment?

While we don’t offer automated online tracking, you can contact your assigned driver or our support team anytime for real-time updates on your vehicle’s status.

Do I need to pay upfront?

No payment is required until a vetted carrier is assigned to your shipment. This ensures you only pay once your vehicle transport is confirmed and scheduled.

Can you ship vehicles to Hawaii, Puerto Rico, or Guam?

Yes, Pine Carrier offers specialized shipping to Hawaii, Puerto Rico, and Guam, managing ports, scheduling, and carriers to ensure your vehicle arrives safely and on time.
